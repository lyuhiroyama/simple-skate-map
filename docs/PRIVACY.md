# Privacy Policy

**Simple Skate Map** (“the app”) is for documenting street architecture and
clips with people you invite. It is not a guidebook. Do not trespass.

**Contact:** lyuhiroyama@gmail.com

## What we collect

When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive an account identifier from
that provider. We store a username you choose, spots you pin (name, notes,
address, map coordinates), photos and videos you upload, group membership, and
chat messages you send. We use your location on device to place pins and to
open the map near you. We do not run a background location tracker.

## How we use it

We use this data only to run the app: show your map, share spots with groups
you join, deliver chat, and enforce safety tools (filter, report, block). We
do not sell your information or show ads.

## Who can see it

Spots you keep to yourself stay private. Spots you share with a group, and
messages in that group, are visible to members of that group. If you report
content, we store the report so we can act on it.

## Keeping it

We keep your data until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes
your profile, messages, and spots you created. Groups you own are transferred
to another member when one exists, or removed if you were the only member.

You can delete your account in the app: Groups → Privacy & account → Delete
account.

## Safety

You can report a message or spot, and block a person so you stop seeing their
content. Chat and spot text is filtered for language we do not allow. Email
support to report a problem or ask us to remove content.

## Children

Simple Skate Map is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from them.

## Contact

Questions, reports, or deletion requests: lyuhiroyama@gmail.com
