DESCRIPTION

Simple Skate Map is for documenting street architecture and clips with people you invite.

Pin a place, add photos or video, and share it with a group. Chat about what you noticed. The map is for looking — not a guidebook. Don’t trespass.

Sign in with Apple or Google. No passwords.

You can report or block people, and delete your account in the app (Groups → Privacy & account).

REVIEW NOTES

Sign in with Apple or Google (no email/password).

UGC: long-press a chat message or group photo to Report or Block.
On someone else’s spot, tap Report or block.
Filtered language is rejected on send.
Privacy, support, and Delete account: Groups → Privacy & account.

The app is for documenting places you admire, not for finding
spots to skate. Do not trespass.
